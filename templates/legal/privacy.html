Privacy Policy

Effective 7 September 2026 · Last updated 7 September 2026

Legal status: Uwitz is currently unincorporated. We are preparing to legally register and operate under Estonia in the foreseeable future.

What we collect

When you create an account or use Uwitz Cards, we collect only what's necessary to provide the service:

  • Account information: Name, email address, username, and password (hashed with bcrypt).
  • SSO data: If you sign in via Entra ID or Irys, we receive your email, display name, and group memberships from the identity provider.
  • Card data: vCard contents, redirect URLs, and card images you upload.
  • Order data: Shipping addresses and payment records (processed via Stripe — we never see your card number).
  • Usage data: Card tap counts (anonymous, no IP or device tracking).

What we don't collect

  • No analytics, tracking, or fingerprinting scripts.
  • No third-party cookies or advertising pixels.
  • No IP addresses or device information beyond what's required for session management.
  • No data sold to or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Provide and maintain the Uwitz Cards service.
  • Process orders and ship physical cards.
  • Send transactional emails (order confirmations, account notifications).
  • Enforce access control and security.

Data storage

All data is stored in MongoDB. Card images are stored on the server filesystem. We apply encryption in transit (TLS) and access controls to protect your data.

Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days. Card images and order records may be retained for legal or operational purposes.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Export your data in a portable format.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@uwitz.org.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via email or a notice on the platform.
