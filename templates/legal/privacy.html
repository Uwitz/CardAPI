4. Special Categories & Children's Data (COPPA)

We do not process special category data (health, biometrics, etc.) under GDPR Art. 9. For children's data:

  • Under 13: We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent (COPPA). Age verification is required at registration.
  • Ages 13–17: Standard data subject rights apply with enhanced protections.
  • Parental Rights: Parents can review, correct, or delete their child's data at any time by contacting privacy@uwitz.org.

8. Automated Decision-Making (GDPR Art. 22)

We do not use solely automated decision-making (including profiling) that produces legal effects or significantly affects you. Any automated processes (e.g., fraud detection) include human review.

9. Privacy by Design & Default (GDPR Art. 25, nFADP Art. 7)

We implement privacy by design and by default:

  • Data Minimization: We collect only data necessary for each purpose.
  • Opt-in by Default: Analytics, marketing, third-party sharing, and non-essential cookies require explicit opt-in.
  • Encryption: TLS 1.3 for data in transit; AES-256 for data at rest.
  • Access Controls: Role-based access, least privilege, audit logging.
  • Automatic Deletion: Data automatically deleted after retention periods.

9. Security Measures (GDPR Art. 32, nFADP Art. 8, PDPA s.24)

  • TLS 1.3 for all connections
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest (MongoDB encryption at rest)
  • Bcrypt for password hashing (cost factor 12)
  • Rate limiting on auth endpoints (5 req/min)
  • Account lockout after 5 failed attempts (30 min)
  • CSRF protection with session-bound tokens
  • Content Security Policy (CSP) headers
  • Regular security assessments

10. Data Breach Notification (GDPR Art. 33-34, nFADP Art. 24, PDPA s.26A)

In the event of a personal data breach:

  • We will notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours (GDPR Art. 33, nFADP Art. 24).
  • Affected individuals will be notified without undue delay if the breach poses high risk (GDPR Art. 34).
  • Breach records are maintained per GDPR Art. 33(5).

11. Data Protection Impact Assessments (GDPR Art. 35, nFADP Art. 22)

We conduct DPIAs for high-risk processing including: NFC card serving, physical card shipping with PII, payment processing via Stripe, and corporate card management. DPIA summaries are available upon request.

12. Records of Processing Activities (GDPR Art. 30, nFADP Art. 12)

We maintain ROPA covering all processing activities. Available to supervisory authorities on request.

13. Cookies and Tracking

We use only essential cookies:

  • uwitz_session — Session authentication (7 days, HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax)
  • uwitz_csrf — CSRF protection (7 days, HttpOnly=false for JS access, Secure, SameSite=Lax)

No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies. Non-essential cookies require your explicit consent.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be communicated via email or platform notice at least 30 days before taking effect. The "Last updated" date above will be revised.
