Everything you need in a card

Three card types for individuals, teams, and dynamic use cases. All powered by NTAG 215 NFC.

Social Contact Card

Your digital vCard with name, email, phone, website, and social profiles. One tap shares your complete contact info — no app required.

Corporate Cards

Branded cards for your entire team. Corporate admins manage all employee cards from a single dashboard with bulk ordering and member invites.

TagLink Cards

Dynamic redirect cards you can update on-the-fly via API. Perfect for iOS Shortcuts, marketing campaigns, or any automation — change where your card points to anytime.

Physical NFC Cards

ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 compliant cards (85.6×54mm) with NTAG 215 chips. Upload your own design or use the default. Printed and shipped to your door.

Manage from Dashboard

Freeze cards, update contact info, change TagLink redirects, manage API keys, upload card images — all from a secure web dashboard.

Email Alerts

Automated notifications for orders, payments, subscription renewals, card status changes, and API key activity. Never miss an update.

How it works

From sign-up to first tap in three steps.

1

Create your card

Sign up and build your digital vCard or TagLink card from the dashboard. Add your name, contact info, social links, or redirect URL.

2
